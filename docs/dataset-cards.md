# Dataset cards — everything shipped under data/samples/

## synthetic_sss — "Synthetic SSS survey (GPS demo)"

- **Type:** synthetically rendered side-scan-style sonar imagery.
- **Contents:** 7 images (480×256): pipe, ghost net ×2, wreck, rock
  (hard negative), cylinder, debris — each drawn with highlight, acoustic
  shadow, speckle, nadir gap and range geometry.
- **Metadata:** fake GPS/heading/altitude along a survey line **explicitly
  labeled synthetic** (manifest `synthetic: true`, violet chip in UI).
- **Ground truth:** pixel-accurate boxes written by the generator
  (`synthetic_sss.json`) for the evaluation harness.
- **License:** none needed — generated by `backend/scripts/synth.py`, no
  third-party data. Do not present as real survey data.
- **Purpose:** demonstrates geolocation, the map, evidence fusion,
  consistency, dimension/shadow estimates and reporting.

## md_fls_watertank — "MD-FLS Watertank (demo subset)"

- **Type:** real forward-looking sonar (ARIS Explorer 3000) images of marine
  debris placed in a test tank.
- **Source:** Marine Debris Forward-Looking Sonar Datasets, M. Valdenegro-Toro
  et al., OCEANS 2025 Brest, doi:10.1109/OCEANS58557.2025.11104623
  (arXiv:2503.22880); labels by D. Singh et al. Repository:
  github.com/mvaldenegro/marine-debris-fls-datasets.
- **Contents:** 24 of 1,868 labelled images (320×480 grayscale) + pixel
  masks + box ground truth parsed from the VOC annotations.
- **License caveat:** the source repository declares **no explicit license**.
  The subset is bundled strictly for internal demo/evaluation. Verify
  licensing before any public release; the full dataset should be re-fetched
  from the authors.
- **Classes (aliased, semantic review v3):** Bottle/Can/Hook/Shampoo-bottle/
  Standing-bottle/Valve → `debris`; **Tire → `tire`** (v3: a tire is a
  toroidal/ring-shaped manufactured object — toroidal and solid-cylinder
  sonar signatures differ in shadow shape and highlight topology, so it is
  **never** used to supervise `cylinder`); Propeller → `structure` (intact
  man-made hard structure, **not** a sunken-vessel wreck semantic);
  Wall → `structure`; **Chain is excluded from detector classes entirely** —
  a chain is rigid metal links, not filament mesh, so it must never supervise
  `net_like`/`ghost_net`; chain crops are used only for natural-vs-artificial
  training and as documented hard negatives.
- **Purpose:** the only real sonar imagery in the demo; used by the
  evaluation harness for P/R/F1 and mask Dice.

## md_fls_watertank_demo — "curated demo" (8 images)

Subset of the above whose confirmations match ground truth well; the
one-click demo survey shown in the SIH presentation. Same attribution and
license caveat.

# Training datasets — data/raw/ + data/training/

Downloaded and converted by `backend/scripts/download_file.py` and
`backend/scripts/convert_datasets.py`. Each produces a backend-compatible
`manifest.json` plus (where labelled) a YOLO export for Ultralytics training.

## ai4shipwrecks — "AI4Shipwrecks (real SSS)"

- **Type:** real **side-scan sonar** waterfall images — the modality of PS 26057.
- **Source:** Sheppard, Sethuraman, Bagoren, Pinnow, Anderson, Havens,
  Skinner (2024), *Machine Learning for Shipwreck Segmentation from Side Scan
  Sonar Imagery*, NOAA Thunder Bay National Marine Sanctuary.
  DOI [10.7302/dmf4-x492](https://doi.org/10.7302/dmf4-x492). 1.13 GB zip,
  file-set id `db78tc698` on Deep Blue.
- **Contents (verified):** 261 labelled images (141 train / 120 test,
  split **by wreck site** — no site leakage), 25 terrain-only negatives
  (`extras/terrain`), 1728 px-wide variable-height greyscale PNGs; binary
  expert masks (0=background, 1=wreck).
- **Derived training data:** 954 `wreck` boxes from connected components of
  masks (min 48 px²); 161 instance-indexed masks exported to `masks/` for
  U-Net; 100 images kept as **labelled negatives** (empty YOLO label files)
  to teach background rejection.
- **License:** **CC-BY 4.0** — attribution required, safe for demo/training.
- **Purpose:** primary real-SSS detection + segmentation training set;
  terrain negatives feed hard-negative mining for the false-positive story.

## sctd — "SCTD 1.0 (mixed sonar)"

- **Type:** 357 high-resolution sonar images mixing side-scan, forward-looking
  and synthetic-aperture sonar; VOC boxes (363 targets).
- **Source:** github.com/MingqiangNing/SCTD (Ning et al.; Zhang et al.,
  IEEE TGRS 2022 doi:10.1109/TGRS.2021.3096011). Classes verified on disk:
  ship 271, aircraft 57, human 35.
- **Mapping:** ship/aircraft → `wreck` (328), human → `debris` (35);
  `raw_class` preserved in the manifest.
- **License caveat:** repo declares **no explicit license** — academic/internal
  use only; verify before any public release.
- **Purpose:** cross-modality detector robustness (SSS/FLS/SAS variety).

## klsg — "SeabedObjects-KLSG (real SSS, classification)"

- **Type:** 447 real side-scan whole-image classification samples:
  **385 ship + 62 airplane** (verified on disk). No bounding boxes.
- **Source:** github.com/huoguanying/SeabedObjects-Ship-and-Airplane-dataset
  ("open for academic use" per README).
- **Use:** natural-vs-artificial classifier training and per-class priors;
  ships/planes are unambiguous artificial targets in real SSS.

## uatd — "UATD (MFLS detection)" *(not downloaded — re-download required)*

- **Planned:** 9,180 multibeam-FLS images, 10 classes with boxes
  (figshare item 21331143, 3.87 GB training zip). figshare serves large
  files with a `202 Accepted` "preparing" phase — download via
  `download_file.py`, which waits it out.
- **Mapping (planned, semantic review v3):** tyre → `tire` (own class,
  never `cylinder`); ball/cube/bucket → `debris` (generic man-made objects).
  **human/ROV/plane → EXCLUDED from detector supervision** — raw labels
  preserved, usable only as contextual/hard-negative examples. A diver is
  not marine debris, and a ROV/plane has no verified wreck or debris
  semantic in this data.
  **Square-cage/circle-cage are NOT mapped to `net_like`** — a rigid cage is
  not fishing-net mesh; they stay raw-labelled for the anomaly pathway and
  future net-specific labelling.
- **License:** open scientific data per figshare item; cite Xie et al. 2022
  (doi:10.3390/s22239180).

### Semantic label review (v3) — final supervised taxonomy

Per-class review against the actual source annotations (no merges made just
to increase sample counts):

| Source label | Source | Count | Final class | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI4 masks (binary wreck) | AI4Shipwrecks | 954 boxes / 161 masks | `wreck` | sunken vessels, real SSS, expert pixel labels |
| ship / aircraft | SCTD | 271 / 57 | `wreck` | sunken ships / aircraft — same wreck semantic |
| **Tire** | MD-FLS | 615 | **`tire`** | **v3: rejected `cylinder`** — a tire is toroidal/ring-shaped, not a generic cylinder; resemblance in sonar does not establish semantic equivalence for supervised detection. Kept as its own supervised class. |
| Propeller | MD-FLS | 197 | `structure` | intact man-made structure, **not** a wreck; never supervise it as `wreck` |
| Wall | MD-FLS | 1,056 | `structure` | tank wall — hard-negative background, kept separate from debris |
| Bottle/Can/Drink-carton/Hook/Shampoo-bottle/Standing-bottle/Valve | MD-FLS | 1,694 | `debris` | generic man-made debris, no closer evidence-backed class (count verified per-XML: 468+347+292+240+171+111+65) |
| **human** | SCTD | 35 | **excluded from supervision** | **v3: rejected `debris`** — a human/diver is not marine debris. Boxes removed from detector training; raw_class preserved for traceability; usable only as contextual/hard-negative examples or a future separate class. |
| **Chain** | MD-FLS | 320 | **hard-negative only** | rigid metal links ≠ filament mesh; never used as `net_like`/`ghost_net` supervision — only as nat-vs-art crops and detector hard-negative |
| **ghost_net, pipe, cylinder** | — | **0** | **not trained** | no genuine labelled examples exist (cylinder: **0 verified examples** after v3 — tire data can never support it); the detector must not claim them — unsupported objects route through the Unknown-Anomaly pathway |

Final detector classes: **`wreck, debris, structure, tire`** (fixed order
= `data/training/*/yolo/classes.txt` = `models/registry.json` `classes`).
`cylinder` is absent by design — **0 verified training examples** — and is
listed in `unsupported_classes` in `training/config.yaml` until genuine
cylinder annotations exist. Earlier exports under the legacy 7-class order
are superseded; regenerate via `convert_datasets.py` /
`package_for_colab.py` / `prepare_sample_data.py`.
Full decision log: `docs/training.md` § taxonomy.
